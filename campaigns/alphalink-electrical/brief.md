# Alphalink Electrical: creative brief (switchboard campaign)

**Objective:** quote requests for switchboard checks/upgrades (KPI: cost per quote request).
**Audience:** homeowners and landlords in Melbourne's south-east with older homes (fuse boxes), plus
people adding an EV charger or aircon.
**Proposition:** *Old fuses, tripping power or a new EV charger? Get your switchboard checked and quoted upfront by a Master Electrician.*
**Landing page:** /service/switchboard-upgrades/ (CTA "Request a quote", "Response in 24hrs or less").

## Angles
| Angle | Insight (VoC) | Promise | Proof | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Safety check | "very old main switch box" | Know if your board is safe | Master Electricians, 15+ yrs | F2 listicle |
| B. Capacity | EV/aircon buyers don't know if the board copes | Check your board can handle the load | Switchboard page service list | F1 PAS-15 |
| C. Trusted sparky | "Jimmy… worked magic", "no disruption" | One local, upfront sparky | 5/5 Google, reviews | F3 testimonial-led |

## Built

## Test plan
Hook A vs Hook B on Reels (9:16), same audience and budget, 5-7 days. Judge on 3-second hook
rate, then cost per quote request. Winner hook → build Angle B (EV capacity) on it.
