## Pains → desires → objections
* **Pains:** old fuse box / "very old main switch box", power tripping, new EV charger/aircon may need more capacity, finding a reliable tradie, disruption (tenants, power off).
* **Desires:** safe home, done right first time, fair upfront price, fast response, tidy work, one trusted sparky for everything.
* **Objections:** "Do I really need a whole new switchboard?" (page answers: *not always*), cost uncertainty, how long the power is off.
