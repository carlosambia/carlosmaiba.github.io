# Stage 2: concepts

Inputs: `research.md`, `brand.json`, `docs/formulas.md`.

1. Define: objective (one KPI), audience (one sentence, specific), single-minded proposition,
   landing page (must exist in the research landing-page table).
2. Write **3 angles** (e.g. safety/fear, convenience/cost, social proof). For each: insight
   (from voice-of-customer quote), promise, proof (from claims ledger), formula (F1-F9).
3. Delegate to **copy-chief**: ≥ 12 hooks across the 12 archetypes in `docs/formulas.md`,
   ≤ 7 words each, using customer language. Then have **creative-director** score them
   (stop-power, specificity, relevance, brand-fit, honesty; 1-5 each).
4. Write `campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`: proposition, angles table, hook bank sorted by score,
   recommended formula + durations + formats, test plan (which 2-3 hooks to A/B).
5. Present the top angle + top 6 hooks to the user and wait for their pick.
