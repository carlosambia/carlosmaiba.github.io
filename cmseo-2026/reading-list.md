# Reading list: web, blog, UX and marketing copy

A shortlist for the team, checked November 2026.

- ★ = start here
- *Classic* = older, but still the reference everyone builds on

## Books

### Web writing, UX writing and content design

- ★ **Content Design**, Sarah Winters (formerly Richards). Writing from user needs. Grew out of the GOV.UK approach.
- ★ **Writing Is Designing**, Michael J. Metts and Andy Welfle. Words as part of the interface.
- **Strategic Writing for UX**, Torrey Podmajersky. Voice charts, plus how to measure whether copy works.
- **Microcopy: The Complete Guide**, Kinneret Yifrah. Buttons, errors, forms and empty states.
- **The Fundamentals of UX Writing**, Apress, Feb 2026. New, with few reviews so far.
- ★ **Writing for Busy Readers**, Todd Rogers and Jessica Lasky-Fink. Research-backed rules for writing people actually read.
- **Smart Brevity**, VandeHei, Allen and Schwartz (Axios). Short, scannable, opinionated.
- **Designing Connected Content**, Carrie Hane and Mike Atherton. Structured content, which links to Alex Moss's agent-ready talk.
- **Don't Make Me Think**, Steve Krug. *Classic.*

### Copywriting and marketing

- ★ **Everybody Writes** (2nd edition), Ann Handley.
- **The Complete Copywriter**, Alan Barker (Kogan Page, 2025 edition). Covers digital and blog writing.
- **They Ask, You Answer**, Marcus Sheridan. Content that answers buyers' real questions. Strong fit for B2B, such as fuel cards.
- **Obviously Awesome**, April Dunford. Positioning: copy only works when the positioning is clear.
- **Breakthrough Advertising**, Eugene Schwartz. *Classic.* Writing for the reader's level of awareness.
- **Ogilvy on Advertising**, David Ogilvy. *Classic.*

### Style and clarity

- ★ **Several Short Sentences About Writing**, Verlyn Klinkenborg. The best book on sentence rhythm, and very useful for voice work.
- **On Writing Well**, William Zinsser. *Classic.*
- **The Sense of Style**, Steven Pinker.
- **Writing with Style**, Lane Greene (The Economist's style guide).
- **Dreyer's English**, Benjamin Dreyer.

### AI era

- **AI-Powered Content Marketing and SEO**, Catherine Seda and Jenny Halasz (Addison-Wesley, late 2025).
- **Co-Intelligence**, Ethan Mollick. How to work with AI as a collaborator.

## Free style guides

These are real-world voice cards, the same idea as the Voice Lab plan.

- ★ **GOV.UK style guide and content design guidance.** The gold standard for plain language. GOV.UK beat Alex Moss's own site for citations in his test.
- **Mailchimp Content Style Guide.** Voice vs tone, explained well.
- **Shopify Polaris**, content guidelines.
- **Atlassian Design System**, voice, tone and writing guidelines.
- **Intuit Content Design.**
- **Microsoft Writing Style Guide** and **Google developer documentation style guide.**

## Blogs, sites and newsletters

### UX and content design

- ★ **Nielsen Norman Group** (nngroup.com). Research on how people actually read online.
- **UX Content Collective** (uxcontent.com). Articles and courses.
- **Content Design London.** Sarah Winters' team.
- **Smashing Magazine.** Its UX writing and content articles.
- **Button**, the content design conference. Talk recordings.

### Copywriting

- ★ **Marketing Examples**, Harry Dry. Short before-and-after copy lessons.
- **Very Good Copy**, Eddie Shleyner. Bite-size lessons on copy and creativity.
- **Copyhackers**, Joanna Wiebe. Research-led conversion copy.
- **Copywriting Course**, Neville Medhora.
- **Total Annarchy**, Ann Handley's newsletter.
- **Really Good Emails.** A gallery of example emails.

### SEO content in the AI-search era

- ★ **Google Search Central:** "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content" and the guidance on writing high-quality reviews. Read the reviews guidance before any mattress-review work.
- **Growth Memo**, Kevin Indig. Data on AI search and content.
- **Ahrefs blog.** Content and AI-search research.
- **One Useful Thing**, Ethan Mollick. Practical AI.
- **HouseFresh**, Gisele Navarro. An independent review site writing openly about what it takes to survive as a reviewer.

## Publications to study as models of good writing

These also work as "comparison writers" in the Voice Lab exams.

- ★ **Wirecutter (NYT).** The benchmark for product reviews: testing method, honest downsides, clear picks. The model for mattress reviews.
- **The Economist.** Tight, plain, confident prose.
- **Axios.** Smart Brevity in practice.
- **The Atlantic** and **The New Yorker.** Long-form voice.
